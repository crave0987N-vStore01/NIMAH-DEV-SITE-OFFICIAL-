About Us

Who We Are

NIMAH DEV STUDIO is a software development studio focused on building modern, high-performance websites, mobile apps, and AI-powered chatbots for businesses in Sri Lanka and beyond. We don't limit ourselves to one type of project — from simple portfolio pages to full booking systems, e-commerce stores, school management systems, and custom business software, we build it the way our client wants it.

Every project starts with listening to what you actually need. Whatever your industry — retail, education, hospitality, healthcare, or something completely different — we design and develop a solution built around your business, not a one-size-fits-all template.

From idea to deployment, we work closely with every client to deliver custom digital solutions that are fast, secure, and built to scale.
